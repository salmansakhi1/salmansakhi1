**Status:** Operational troubleshooting  
**Technologies:** Wireshark, Cisco IOS, MikroTik, pfSense, DHCP, ARP, TDR, SFP

## Overview

Examples of structured diagnostic methods for routing, physical connectivity, DHCP, NAT and VPN problems.

## Reference architecture

Client → access switch → routed gateway/firewall → upstream network; observations at each hop.

This is a high-level explanation, not an export of any employer or customer network.

## Engineering areas

Establish symptoms, verify Layer 1/2 state, inspect VLAN and DHCP behavior, check routes/NAT/firewall states, collect packet captures, test a specific hypothesis, and document changes.

## Supporting evidence to add

Anonymized symptom/log/output → hypothesis → test → confirmed resolution. Unverified hypotheses must remain labeled as such.

## Disclosure

This document is a professional case-study outline. Details that have not been independently verified must not be presented as completed results. Never publish corporate IP addresses, passwords, private configurations, internal identifiers, VPN keys, or client-sensitive information.
